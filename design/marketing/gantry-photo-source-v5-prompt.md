# Photographic source plate

Generator: built-in imagegen. Only the blank monitor photography is generated; app UI is composited deterministically from native-source exports.

Use case: product-mockup. Create one photorealistic product-photography background plate for a Gantry software campaign. This plate will receive REAL screenshots composited later, so do not draw ANY software, text, symbols, icons, logos, status bar, windows, printer cards, or placeholder controls.

Reference image 1 (gantry.png): STYLE ONLY. Match its exquisite dark screen close-up photography, black glass, fine warm orange rim light, restrained industrial finish and slight diagonal framing. Reference image 2: STYLE ONLY, for its convincing macro display-edge textures and warm grazing reflections. Do not reproduce the edge dock, progress rings, text, or layouts from either reference.

Landscape 3:2, maximum available quality. Single shot, NOT a collage. A premium thin dark-metal desktop monitor photographed close-up in a pitch-dark studio. Its screen is switched on but displays ONLY a perfectly plain deep charcoal-black surface, nearly uniform, suitable for deterministic screenshot insertion. Show all FOUR corners of the active screen as an unambiguous plane, with a narrow black glass bezel and a subtle warm amber-orange rim highlight tracing the left outer edge and top edge. The active screen should occupy about 80% of the image, centered, not too much empty background. Three-quarter camera angle with gentle perspective, slightly rotated counterclockwise about 5 degrees: top screen edge slopes gently upwards to the right. Keep moderate perspective, not extreme foreshortening; screenshots must remain readable after insertion. Aim for active-screen corners approximately (190,140), (1400,60), (1380,860), (170,940) in a 1536×1024 image. Nothing crosses in front of the display. No reflections obscuring the screen. Sharp focus across the screen, shallow falloff on outer metal edges. Tiny real dust and microtexture on the bezel only, elegant and subtle. Natural photographic dynamic range, soft studio speculars, rich blacks. A barely visible dark stand or desk below, no clutter, no keyboard required. No tablet, phone, hands or other product. The mood and material beauty should unmistakably belong to the same high-end campaign as the references, NOT a flat illustration or wireframe. No text whatsoever.
